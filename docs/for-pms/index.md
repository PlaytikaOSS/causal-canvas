# For Product Managers

Causal Canvas reads existing data and proposes which levers — features, campaigns, mechanics — most likely move a metric you care about, like churn or spend. This section is written for the decisions you can make, the limits of what you should trust, and the conversations to have with your data team. No formulas.

- [Overview](overview.md) — what it does for product decisions, in 10 minutes.
- [Questions to ask your data team](questions-to-ask.md) — a checklist before acting on a Causal Canvas output.
- [Glossary](glossary.md) — plain-language definitions of the terms you'll hear.
